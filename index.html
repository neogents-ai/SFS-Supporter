The system was never built for us.

Most life and business management tools were built for people who already have the blueprint. FBAs juggle wealth gaps, generational trauma, and underfunded futures — with no AI-powered command center designed specifically for us.

  • ▶No unified tool that handles business finances, family budgeting, and personal growth in one place
  • ▶Generic AI assistants trained on data that erases Black wealth-building context
  • ▶Entrepreneurs flying blind — no weekly audit, no clear KPIs, no strategic advisor on call
  • ▶Community wealth knowledge locked inside consultants who charge $500/hr

Meet NORI and Friends.

An AI system that scans your data, dispatches specialized agents, and delivers two weekly outputs: a Business Dashboard every Monday and a Life Audit Brief every Sunday. Each character runs its own roster of agents — tap a card to see them.
